## The walls, with numbers

```
players      76972 / cap 123825  highest id 260545  ids left 1598
teams         2886 / cap 4800    national 211
competitions   255 / 255  free 0
regulations     403 / 1024
slots          158 of 250 used by the world file
```

- **Players**: the 18-bit id (262,144) is the wall, not the array. The game's own table is
  sparse (29,492 players over 184,461 ids): **150,776 free ids** that the builder never used.
  `playeredit.free_ids` and `--ids free` (tools) and `FL26_FREE_PLAYER_IDS=1` (`lbplayers`) take
  them. The Build's own new-player cursor still starts above the game's highest -- next step.
- **Competitions**: three byte reads of the id are known (census `0x1414f1500`, entry
  `0x1414f160f`, regulation lookup `0x141eee27c`); ids above 255 need all of them plus the data
  writers.
- **Leagues**: 250 slots (158 used) and 255 competition ids; the twins are the lever if ML
  accepts two regulations on one id.
